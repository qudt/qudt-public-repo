Notable improvements in this Release.

- Cleaned up descriptions that were unneccesarily tagged as either HTML or LatexString
- Added untagged (i.e. language-agnostic) labels for all units, quantity kinds, constants and prefixes. Suggested by @berliner007
- A variety of new meteorological quantity kinds and associated units (e.g. Geopotential). Thanks to @miki4iaml
- Expansion of the concepts of "parts per ..." beyond just mole fraction, to mass fraction and volume fraction

...plus a variety of fixes due to ambiguity, erroneous references to external standards, and more. See the CHANGELOG for details.
