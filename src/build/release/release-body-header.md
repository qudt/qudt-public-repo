There are several notable improvements in this Release.

- QUDT now models Equivalent quantity kinds and units in a consistent manner, documented in [our wiki](https://github.com/qudt/qudt-public-repo/wiki/Equivalents-Measures-Units-and-Quantity-Kinds).
- We have begun the rollout of automatic labelling of new Issues to aid in the prioritization of issue handling by categorizing them by area, effort, impact and type.
- Numerous other tweaks for consistency improvement have been made.
